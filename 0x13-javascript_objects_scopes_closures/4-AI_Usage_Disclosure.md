## AI Usage Disclosure for Student Submissions

### Policy

Students may use AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Copilot) for **brainstorming and idea generation only**—for example, exploring possible topics, angles, or approaches before writing.

AI tools may **not** be used to generate any part of the final submission itself, including drafted text, code, images, or other content that is turned in as the student's own work.

Any AI use, even brainstorming, must be disclosed on the form below and submitted with the assignment. Undisclosed use, or use beyond brainstorming, will be treated as a violation of the Academic Integrity Policy.
